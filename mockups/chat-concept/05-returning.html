7 Profile tab → one sheet
Everything from today's Profile tab that still matters fits in a sheet off the avatar: subscription, brand, notification preferences, support. Stats and credit counters are gone — nothing to count anymore.
Drafts disappear as a conceptToday an interrupted wizard needs a draft-save banner. A thread is always resumable mid-sentence — the café owner waiting on her opening date just… replies when she knows it.
Grid view staysThe ▦ toggle shows the visual gallery for "I remember what it looked like" recall. Threads and grid are two views of the same projects.
Trust boundaryBrand memory and proactive nudges must be visible and controllable (the "learned from 12 designs" label, per-item delete, notification toggles) or they flip from delightful to creepy.
