5 Export — plus a retention hook
"Make this monthly" turns one job into a recurring one: each month the AI drafts the next issue from a quick Q&A ("what are June's specials?"). Detail in Journey 5.
Why newsletters fit chat (and not a wizard)Documents have variable structure — N sections, M pages, recurring issues. A wizard would need a screen per section type; a conversation handles "add a volunteer spotlight under the tips" for free.
Scope assumptionThis is a designed document (PDF/print/images), not an email-sending platform — no subscriber lists or delivery infra. "Email-ready images" covers the common small-org workflow of pasting into Gmail/Mailchimp. A send integration is a separate, later bet.
Monetization placementNewsletters are a Pro-only feature — a strong upgrade trigger for exactly the church/fitness/restaurant users who need them monthly. Free users can build one and see a watermarked preview; export asks for Pro (Journey 6).
