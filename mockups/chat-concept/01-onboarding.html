Today's onboarding asks for role, categories, language, visual style, mood, colors, brand kit and more — 12 screens before the first flyer. In the chat-first version, the app asks one question, then drops the user straight into a conversation where the first flyer is the onboarding.

4 Onboarding by doing
Example chips are tailored to the chosen role (church examples here). The first flyer is the tutorial — no workflow demo screen needed.
What got deletedRole grid (kept, as the single question) · category preferences · language picker · visual style picker · mood picker · color picker · AI explainer · workflow demo · brand kit setup · sample showcase. Brand setup moves into conversation (Journey 5) and preferences are inferred from behavior.
Why this mattersEvery preference screen is a drop-off point before the user has seen any value. The current app spends its best attention-minutes on forms; this version spends them on the user's first real flyer.

B1 Hard(ish) paywall after value screen
Maximizes trial starts per install; risks losing users who need to see a real flyer first. The "3 free designs" escape keeps it testable rather than absolute.
Trade-offRecommended default: soft start (Journey A) — let the magic moment sell the subscription at export time (Journey 6). Trial-first converts harder per install but FlyGen's wow moment is cheap to give away: one generation costs cents and sells the product better than any feature list.
