7 Winback with context
Lapsed users return to a designer who remembers them — projects intact, a relevant seasonal reason to create, and a discount (App Store win-back offers support this natively).
Why subscription beats credits for this app
Chat is iteration-heavy. Credits charge 10 per refine — taxing the core behavior. Subscriptions make "try it warmer" free, so the product can encourage it.
The needs are recurring. Weekly bulletins, monthly newsletters, seasonal promos — recurring jobs justify recurring billing in the user's head.
Failures stop costing users money. Garbled-text retries today burn paid credits; under Pro they're invisible. Trust goes up.
Revenue becomes predictable — and Apple's subscription tooling (trials, win-backs, grace periods) replaces hand-rolled promo-pack logic.
The economics to verify"Unlimited" + ~cents-per-image generation needs a fair-use ceiling (generous, e.g. soft cap with priority queue), cheaper draft renders for concepts, and premium quality only on finals. A heavy user must stay profitable at $4.17/mo — this is the one number to model before committing.
Migrating credit holdersExisting purchased credits must convert fairly — e.g. every 100 unspent credits → 1 month of Pro. One-time mapping, announced in-app; nobody loses paid value.
