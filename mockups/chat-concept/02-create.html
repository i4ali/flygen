Where the 9 wizard steps went
1 Category → auto-detected from the sentence (all 15 categories)
2 Text fields → extracted; gaps filled by one follow-up question
3–5 Style · Mood · Colors → pick a concept direction; taste is learned over time
6 Format → smart default (4:5), every other size one chip away
7 QR & Logo → "＋" attach or brand memory (Journeys 3 & 5)
8 Smart extras → AI suggests photos/elements proactively in-thread
9 Review → the conversation is the review
Time to first flyerWizard: ~3–4 minutes of tapping through 9 screens. Chat: ~60–90 seconds, most of it generation time you'd pay either way.
Languages stay first-classType in Urdu, Arabic, Spanish or Chinese and the assistant replies — and designs — in that language (RTL included), reusing the existing 5-language prompt system. See Journey 5 for it in action.
Risk to watchOpen-ended chat can intimidate blank-page users. That's why chips, example prompts and the concept carousel keep every step tappable — typing is always optional (Journey 7 compares models).
