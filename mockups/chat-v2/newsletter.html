Why newsletters earn the subscription

A flyer is a one-off; a newsletter is a commitment. By proposing structure, drafting copy and offering to recur, the assistant turns a dreaded monthly chore into a five-minute review — and gives someone a concrete reason to keep paying month after month. This is the surface area the wizard could never reach.

Last stop: pricing — how the free tier, the weekly + monthly plans and the credit-to-subscription migration work. Documents are CSS stand-ins; the flow is the point.
