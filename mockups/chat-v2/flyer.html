Journey A · the hero flow

Create a flyer with a trained eye.

One conversation replaces all nine wizard steps. The assistant parses your sentence, asks only what it can't infer, then — before it generates anything — plans the flyer like a professional would, running the checklist a good fundraiser flyer needs and saying what it's optimising for. You approve the plan, then refine the result by talking or tapping.

Where the nine wizard steps went — and what got added

Type & category → inferred. Text fields → parsed. Style, mood & colours → three tappable directions. Format → recommended with a reason. QR & logo → asked for when relevant. Review & generate → you approve a real design plan first. The wizard only ever collected inputs; the new "plans it like a pro" step adds the thing it never had — judgement.
