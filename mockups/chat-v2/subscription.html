Migrating today's credit buyers

Nobody loses what they paid for. On launch, remaining credits convert to free Pro time (e.g. each unused credit → a day of unlimited), so a stocked-up user lands inside the subscription already. Past purchasers get a grandfathered trial extension, and the old credit packs simply retire — a one-way door, communicated up front.
